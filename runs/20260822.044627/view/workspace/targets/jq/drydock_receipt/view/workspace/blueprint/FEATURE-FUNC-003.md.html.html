<h2>Workflow</h2>
<p>Function definitions introduce named filters into lexical scope and support recursive evaluation.</p>
<ul><li><code>def name: FILTER;</code> defines a zero-arity function.</li><li>Parameterized definitions support filter and value parameters.</li><li>Definitions are resolved lexically and may refer to themselves recursively.</li><li>Redefinition replaces only the matching arity for subsequent references.</li><li>Function calls preserve generator ordering, closures, and backtracking.</li></ul>
