<requirement name="core-values" stories="core-values">

</requirement>
<requirement name="operators-type-behavior" stories="operators">

</requirement>

<requirement name="functions-and-bindings" stories="functions-bindings">

</requirement>

</requirement>
<requirement name="numbers-and-edge-cases" stories="numeric-builtins">
`nan`, `infinite`, integer/float equality, large literals, and the `have_decnum` builtin — return `false` from it and the corpus takes its non-decNumber branch, which native floats satisfy.
</requirement>
