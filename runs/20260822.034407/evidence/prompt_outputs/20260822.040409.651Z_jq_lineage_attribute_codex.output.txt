<requirement name="values-indexing-and-construction" stories="core-002">

</requirement>
<requirement name="operators-and-comparisons" stories="core-003">
Some jq operators (for instance, `+`) do different things depending on the type of their arguments (arrays, numbers, etc.).
</requirement>

</requirement>
<requirement name="assignment" stories="paths-003">

</requirement>
<requirement name="conditionals-and-error-control" stories="control-001">

</requirement>
<requirement name="reduction-and-iteration" stories="control-002">

</requirement>
<requirement name="recursion" stories="control-003">
The `recurse(f)` function allows you to search through a recursive structure, and extract interesting data from all levels.
</requirement>
